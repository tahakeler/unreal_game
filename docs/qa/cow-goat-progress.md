# Cow + Goat (feature/cow-goat) progress

## Built
- `Scripts/AI/LivestockTuning.cs` (SO, `Resources/Data/Critters/LivestockTuning.asset`): moo, headbutt, climb, graze knobs.
- `Scripts/AI/LivestockRules.cs`: pure rules (headbutt gate/connect/impulse, climb band, perch exit, moo interval, lift).
- `Scripts/AI/CowBehaviour.cs`: moo every 20 s +/- 4 (Critter noise 0.5) + complaint moo on grab.
- `Scripts/AI/GoatBehaviour.cs`: headbutt (0.6 s replicated telegraph + bleat noise, then `IKnockable.ApplyImpulse` 1100 design units = knockdown, never KO, dodgeable), climb/perch (hop onto 0.5-2.2 m tops, bored after 45 s, jumps down when threatened), graze (lures to loose Crop/Product goods, bites = `IGoods.ApplyBruise`, Nature blame).
- `CritterBrain.ExternalHoldUntil` (AI-owned, additive): pauses Idle/Wander target picking during signature tasks.
- `CritterAssetBuilder`: Cow (Quaternius Cow.fbx, 2.1 m, Jersey brown) and Goat (Quaternius Sheep.fbx stand-in, 1.05 m, off-white) specs + personalities; LODGroup on every critter prefab.
- Muddlebury: `CritterSpawner_Cow`/`_Goat` markers (World's `BuildMuddlebury.Markers` + `LivestockSceneHook.AddMuddleburyMarkers` patch of the saved scene).
- Audio: `SFX_Critter_Cow_Moo`, `SFX_Critter_Goat_Bleat` appended to `AudioEvents.All`; `NoiseRouting.TryRoute(e, critterKey, ...)` species overload (old bool overload kept); `AudioService` passes goods id; placeholders generated by `make_audio_placeholders.py` (existing outputs byte-identical).

- No `Goods_Cow/Goat` assets (Economy-owned): CritterGoods fallback stubs carry $150/0.7 and $70/0.5.
