# HARVEST PANIC — Early Beta

*Draft for the owner and community-manager to review. Version proposed: 0.9.0 (see the gate report). Not yet published.*

## Highlights

Muddlebury is open for business. Up to four farmers share one wobbly farm. You haul crates, pumpkins and very
uncooperative pigs over the bridge to market, then try to make the Landlord's rent by Sunday. This beta adds a full
week to the game: an evening shop, weather, nights you would rather not be outside for, a Landlord with rules, and a
camcorder that saves the moment it all went wrong.

## New in this beta

### A full farm week
Five working days, then Sunday rent. Each evening the farm shop opens. Spend the day's takings on upgrades that carry
over to the next day, and the next week, such as Rubber Boots, Better Nets, Padded Crates and a reinforced bridge.
If you come up short on Sunday you can sell upgrades back as Collateral. If that is still not enough, you are evicted.

### Continue Farm
The game saves your farm at every evening shop and after rent is paid. Pick **Continue Farm** from the main menu to
carry on where you left off. If the game is closed at the wrong moment, it falls back to the save before that, so you
lose at most one day.

### Weather
Every morning brings Clear, Rain, Fog, Heatwave or Storm, and each one changes how the day plays. The morning card
tells you what is coming.

### Nights
When the sun goes down, Mr. Stitches walks the fields and foxes come for the coop. The lantern posts help.

### The Landlord
He turns up for inspections, sets a new rule each week (enjoy Quiet Hours), and collects the rent in person on Sunday.
He counts it twice, slowly.

### Cows and goats
Two new animals, each with a personality. Cows are heavy and stubborn. Goats headbutt, climb things they should not
climb, and eat things they should not eat.

### Camcorder
The farm records the last moments before a disaster. Watch the replay from any angle, slow it down, and save the best
shot as a picture. The day's receipt names the Moment of the Day.

### Grandpa's tapes
New farmers get short VHS tapes from Grandpa that teach grabbing, carrying and selling in the first few minutes. You
can skip them, turn them off, and replay them later.

### Settings and accessibility
You can rebind every key, read captions for important sounds, switch on a colour-blind palette, and change comfort
settings: head bob, FOV and camera shake. Controls are listed in the pause menu.

### Sound
New impacts, footsteps, coin sounds, critter barks, a market bell and music that changes with the time of day.
Some sounds are still placeholders.

### Credits
A new **Credits** screen in the main menu lists the team and every free asset and library we use, with their licences.

## Improvements

- The farm renders a lot faster. Muddlebury now uses about a twelfth of the draw calls it used before.
- Less stutter. Several per-frame hitches from the HUD, weather and night systems have been removed.
- The night is darker and moonlit, and the lantern gives off stronger light.
- Finding games: on Windows, Steam lobbies and invites work through the Steam overlay. When Steam is not available
  the game uses your local network, and the menu shows which one you are on.

## Known issues

We know about these and are working on them:

- **Rent in week 1 may be very hard to make.** We are reviewing how much a normal day can earn.
- **Some goods dropped in the Sale Pen do not sell.** We are investigating. Try dropping them in again, nearer the
  middle of the pen.
- **The prize pumpkin can arrive already bruised** at the start of a day.
- **The Landlord sometimes says nothing** during his visits.
- **Voice chat is not available yet.** Use text chat, shouts and pings in the meantime.
- **The macOS build is not yet signed by Apple.** Until it is, macOS may refuse to open the game the first time.
  Right-click the game and choose Open.
- **On macOS, Steam lobbies and invites do not work yet.** Mac players can host and join games on the same local
  network for now. Playing between Mac and Windows over Steam will follow once this is fixed.
- Some music and sounds are placeholders.
